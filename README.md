# Pharma Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes pharmaceutical sales data using Microsoft Excel.

The goal was to explore sales performance across products, regions, customer types, sales representatives, manufacturers, and quarters, and turn the analysis into business insights and recommendations.

This project was created as part of my Business Analyst portfolio.

## Business Questions

The analysis focused on questions such as:

- Which regions generate the highest revenue?
- Which drugs generate the highest revenue?
- Which drugs have the highest revenue per unit?
- How does revenue change across quarters?
- Which customer types generate the most revenue?
- Which sales representatives have the highest and lowest sales performance?
- How do drugs perform across different regions and quarters?
- Which manufacturers generate the highest revenue?
- How do customers and customer types contribute to sales?

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Slicers
- Basic Excel calculations

## Data Preparation

The raw sales data was prepared in Excel before performing the analysis.

The preparation included:

- Checking for missing or blank values
- Checking for duplicate records
- Standardizing data where required
- Checking and correcting data types
- Formatting revenue and units sold appropriately
- Creating a Revenue per Unit metric

## Analysis Performed

The following analyses were performed using PivotTables and PivotCharts:

- Revenue and units sold by region
- Revenue by drug
- Revenue per unit by region
- Revenue per unit by drug
- Revenue by sales representative
- Top 5 and bottom 5 sales representatives
- Revenue by quarter
- Revenue by region and quarter
- Revenue by region, drug, and quarter
- Revenue by manufacturer and drug
- Revenue and units sold by customer type
- Revenue by customer and drug

## Key Findings

- South America generated the highest total revenue among the regions.
- Africa had the lowest revenue per unit among the regions.
- Drug B generated the highest overall revenue.
- Drug A generated the highest overall revenue per unit.
- Pharmacy generated the highest total revenue among customer types.
- Supermarkets generated the lowest total revenue and lowest units sold.
- Revenue increased from Q1 to Q4, with Q4 generating the highest revenue.
- Company V generated the highest revenue among the manufacturers.
- James Smith generated the highest overall revenue among the sales representatives.
- Drug B showed particularly strong Q4 performance in South America, Asia, and Europe.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the observed results:

- Continue focusing on the strong sales performance observed in South America while monitoring other regions for growth opportunities.
- Plan inventory and sales resources ahead of Q4 based on the observed quarterly revenue trend.
- Continue monitoring Drug B because it generated the highest overall revenue.
- Maintain focus on Pharmacy customers while exploring opportunities to improve performance in lower-revenue customer segments.
- Review the performance of lower-performing regions and customer segments to understand potential areas for improvement.
- Further investigate the lower revenue per unit observed in Africa and Online Pharmacy before making strategic decisions.

These recommendations are based on the patterns observed in the dataset and do not assume specific causes without further investigation.
